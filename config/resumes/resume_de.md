# Candidate Profile & Matching Criteria

## Target Preferences (Hard Filters)
* **Target Titles:** Senior Data Engineer, Data Engineer, ETL Developer
* **Preferred Work Setup:** Remote, Hybrid, or On-site (United States)
* **Target Compensation:** Market rate for 7+ years of experience
* **Preferred Tech Stack:** Apache Spark, Python, SQL, Snowflake, Airflow, and dbt
* **Dealbreaker Roles:** Roles that do not align with modern cloud data warehousing or lakehouse architectures.
